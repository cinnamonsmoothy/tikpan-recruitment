Candidate privacy notice.

How Tikpan Recruitment Group Ltd collects, uses and protects candidate information.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

Tikpan Recruitment Group Ltd is a recruitment business registered in Scotland under company number SC904648. Our registered office is 10 Sycamore Row, Fraserburgh, Aberdeenshire, Scotland, AB43 9AF. For the purposes of UK data-protection law, we are the controller of the personal information described in this notice.

Information we may collect

When you contact us, apply for a vacancy or ask us to help you find work, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your CV, employment history, qualifications, skills and professional interests.
  • Information about the roles, locations, working arrangements and salary expectations you are interested in.
  • Interview notes, communications and feedback relating to applications or placements.
  • Information needed to confirm identity, eligibility to work, references or compliance requirements where relevant.
  • Any other information you choose to provide to us.

Please avoid sending information that is not relevant to your application. If a role requires us to process special-category or criminal-offence information, we will only do so where the law permits and appropriate safeguards are in place.

How we obtain your information

We normally receive information directly from you. We may also receive it from referees, recruitment platforms, publicly available professional profiles, employers or another person who recommends you. If you send us a speculative CV, we will handle it in accordance with this notice.

Why we use your information

We use candidate information to communicate with you, assess your suitability, identify and discuss opportunities, introduce you to prospective employers where appropriate, manage applications and placements, improve our recruitment services, maintain business records and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

Our lawful bases

Depending on the circumstances, we process information because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, to perform a contract, to comply with a legal obligation, or because we and our clients have legitimate interests in operating an effective recruitment service. Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it separately and you may withdraw it.

Who we may share information with

We may share relevant information with prospective or current employers, referees, professional advisers, technology and administrative service providers, and public authorities where required by law. We only share what is reasonably necessary and do not sell candidate information.

International transfers

If information needs to be transferred outside the United Kingdom, we will use an appropriate legal transfer mechanism and safeguards required by data-protection law.

How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably required for recruitment, placement, legal, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes. Retention depends on the nature of our relationship, whether an application or placement is active, applicable legal requirements and whether you would reasonably expect us to retain your details for future opportunities. Information that is no longer required is securely deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your information, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to its use, request portability, or withdraw consent. You may contact us directly at info@tikpanrecruitment.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Questions and complaints

Please contact Tikpan Recruitment Group Ltd at info@tikpanrecruitment.co.uk if you have a question or concern about your information. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
